## Deep review method

For each proposition:

1. identify the governed object and computational owner;
2. identify current maturity and lifecycle dimensions;
3. identify the applicable evidence class;
4. inspect the public artifact, source anchor, test fixture, observation, or receipt;
5. verify exact identity, provenance, and source-review date;
6. compare the proposition to the evidence scope; and
7. extend into controlled diligence when the proposition requires protected source or execution evidence.

## Evidence progression

Component qualification and complete-system benchmarking are distinct evidence classes. Component tests establish bounded component behavior. Integrated comparative benchmarking can declare method, latency, throughput, compute, model/API calls, token use, replayability, withholding/failure behavior, evidence, human review, output quality, and cost.
