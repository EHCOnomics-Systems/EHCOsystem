# Terminology

A short glossary for terms used throughout the public technical record. For the full category definition and operating model, see [Instantiated AI](../architecture/INSTANTIATED-AI.md).

## Instantiated AI
The architectural category in which intelligence operates inside explicit computational conditions for identity, authority, state, memory, source, permissible range, evidence, release, and consequence.

## EHCOsystem
EHCOnomics' implementation of the Instantiated AI architecture across a governed technology estate.

## EHCO AI-OS
The Tier One Runtime system identity of EHCOsystem. See the [accepted Runtime evidence route](../runtime/README.md).

## INSTANTIATED_EHCO_RUNTIME
The owner of Tier One Runtime authority and Runtime state. Public repository artifacts do not substitute for this authority domain.

## Computational standing
The system-recognized condition under which a participant, source, memory, instruction, operation, or result is eligible to participate within a particular computation. See the [canonical architecture definition](../architecture/INSTANTIATED-AI.md) and the [public claim registry](../assurance/PUBLIC-CLAIM-REGISTRY.json).

## Accepted standing 52/53
The accepted numerical Runtime standing corridor under the EHCO standing framework. It is not a benchmark percentage, deployment percentage, component maturity percentage, or public-release percentage. The public claim registry carries the current public interpretation and evidence route.

## Source standing
The recognized condition under which a source may be relied upon for a particular computation, including its provenance, authority, scope, applicability, and present-use status.

## Memory standing
The recognized condition under which retained information may participate in present computation, preserving relevant creator, authority, applicability, time, supersession, correction, and current-use boundaries.

## Permissible range
The set of operations presently available under the governing identity, authority, state, source, evidence, and other applicable conditions.

## Evidence
The material that supports a recognized technical or computational condition. Different propositions are owned by different evidence domains; repository publication does not collapse those distinctions.

## Consequence
The governed effect path available after applicable conditions, evidence, and release requirements are satisfied.

## Withholding
A typed governed outcome in which a requested operation or result is not released because applicable conditions are not satisfied or remain unresolved. Withholding is not equivalent to an execution error.

## Continuity
The preservation of governed identity, state, source, memory, evidence, and related conditions across time, transitions, correction, recovery, or later computation.

## Mutual computational proof
A bounded proof relationship in which material proof-relevant conditions of a governed interaction can be established and correlated for the system/provider and the person or organization relying on it, subject to applicable authority, disclosure, privacy, evidence, and proprietary-custody boundaries.

## Public verifier
Code that checks the public repository representation and intentionally public proof objects. Start with [`VERIFY.md`](../VERIFY.md) or [Public Verification](../verification/README.md).

## Proprietary implementation
Controlled implementation source and mechanics that have not been selected for public distribution.

## Public proof
A bounded, intentionally public computation that lets an independent reviewer test a published proposition without receiving proprietary implementation source.
