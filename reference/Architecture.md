The main distinctions are straightforward:

- `INSTANTIATED_EHCO_RUNTIME` owns Runtime authority and Runtime state.
- `EHCO_DOCKER_PORTABILITY` is the `PRIMARY_ACCESSIBLE_RUNTIME_PROJECTION`.
