## Review method

1. begin with the current Full Flex Runtime evidence route for present Runtime posture;
2. identify the technical proposition;
3. identify the governed object and owner;
4. inspect the applicable source/evidence class;
5. verify exact identity, scope and time;
6. inspect qualification or observed execution evidence when the proposition concerns behavior; and
7. preserve the evidence class and source relationship in the conclusion.
