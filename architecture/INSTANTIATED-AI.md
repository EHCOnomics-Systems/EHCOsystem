# Instantiated AI

> **Instantiated AI creates the computational conditions under which artificial intelligence may lawfully operate.**

**Instantiated AI is an artificial intelligence architecture in which the computational conditions governing identity, authority, state, memory, source, scope, permissible action, evidence, release, and consequence are established independently of the intelligence they govern and before consequential operation.**

The governing system establishes the operating conditions. Intelligence interprets, reasons, explains, proposes, and performs work inside those established conditions.

## EHCOsystem as an Instantiated AI ecosystem

**EHCOsystem is EHCOnomics' Instantiated AI ecosystem.**

Within EHCOsystem:

- **EHCO AI-OS** is the realized Tier One Runtime foundation; **`INSTANTIATED_EHCO_RUNTIME`** owns Tier One Runtime authority and Runtime state, including the governing relationships for scope, recognized state, transition, consequence, continuity, persistence, withholding, release, correction, recovery, and Runtime-originated proof;
- **downstream governed components** own deterministic language, range/reasoning, retrieval/evidence, relationship, coordination, research, and domain-application capabilities;
- **Tier Three** owns interfaces and projections that make governed information visible and usable; and
- **public evidence and technical diligence** provide source-bound inspection routes for material technical propositions.

Persistent component identity, application/service realization, and scoped Runtime participation are independently governed dimensions. Runtime participation is established only through the applicable owning Runtime evidence for the relevant corridor and time.

## Representation and instantiation

A representation describes a condition. **Instantiation establishes the condition computationally.**

An instantiated system can answer which identity is operating, which authority applies, which role and scope govern the operation, which state is recognized, which sources and memories have standing, which rules apply, which operations are available, which evidence supports the decision, and which release and consequence paths are active.

This creates two complementary layers:

| Layer | Function |
|---|---|
| Representation | describes, proposes, interprets, explains |
| Instantiation | establishes governing computational conditions |

## Lawful operation

Within this architecture, **lawful operation** means operation under computational conditions that hold standing in the governing system.

Those conditions can encode organizational authority, contractual obligations, legislation and regulation, roles, policies, user consent, security requirements, operating procedures, resource boundaries, evidence requirements, and other applicable rules. Legal and regulatory determinations retain their applicable professional, institutional, and evidentiary owners.

## Dependency inversion

Instantiated AI places governing computational reality upstream of intelligence:

```text
Identity
  ↓
Authority
  ↓
State
  ↓
Source / Memory Standing
  ↓
Applicable Rules
  ↓
Permissible Range
  ↓
Intelligence
  ↓
Release
  ↓
Consequence
  ↓
Proof / Continuity
```

This dependency order lets intelligence operate with substantial capability while identity, authority, state, evidence, and consequence remain governed computational properties.

## Computational standing

**Computational standing** is the system-recognized condition under which a participant, source, memory, instruction, operation, or result is eligible to participate within a particular computation.

Capability and standing answer different questions:

- **Capability:** what work can this component perform?
- **Standing:** under which established conditions may that work participate in governed consequence?

The same separation applies to retrieval and source authority, stored memory and memory standing, proposals and accepted state transitions, and component capability and Runtime participation.

## Standing Baseline

A **Standing Baseline** is the maintained computational representation of identities, authority, relationships, state, evidence, and operating conditions from which standing can be determined.

Durable conditions can be established and maintained, and later changes can be evaluated against that recognized baseline. EHCO AI-OS carries an accepted numerical standing baseline of **52/53** through its applicable Runtime authority and proof path.

## Source and memory standing

Instantiated AI gives source and memory their own governed standing chains.

```text
source → provenance → authority → scope → standing → reliance
```

A governed memory environment can preserve creator, authority, applicability, time, supersession, correction, and present-use standing. This creates durable continuity while keeping present computation source-aware and evidence-aware.

## Intelligence proposals and consequential state

Intelligence supplies interpretation, generation, search, reasoning, planning, and proposals. The governing system evaluates those proposals against identity, authority, state, source, evidence, permissible range, release, and consequence conditions.

The resulting architecture separates **intelligence production** from **governed state transition**, allowing powerful intelligence to operate inside a computationally accountable environment.

## Model independence

The governing reality survives replacement of the intelligence model. Identity, authority, organizational state, source legitimacy, memory legitimacy, operating rules, proof, and continuity remain properties of the instantiated system.

This provides a strong architectural test: **the model supplies intelligence; the instantiated system supplies the conditions governing that intelligence.**

## Relationship to familiar AI controls

Instantiated AI composes naturally with familiar control families:

- **Permissions and IAM** establish access rights.
- **Guardrails** constrain generated behavior.
- **Prompt engineering** structures model interaction.
- **RAG** supplies retrieved context and evidence candidates.
- **Containerization** supplies execution environments and portability.
- **Agent frameworks** supply planning, tools, memory, delegation, and action mechanics.
- **Instantiated AI** establishes standing, authority, recognized state, source/memory legitimacy, evidence, permissible range, release, and consequence around those capabilities.

A system can therefore be generative, predictive, conversational, agentic, autonomous, deterministic, or hybrid while also implementing Instantiated AI conditions.
