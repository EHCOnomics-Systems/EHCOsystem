# Instantiated Proof Range

EHCO AI-OS is the realized Tier One Runtime with accepted standing **52/53**. The accepted public proof range covers governed relationships among authority, standing, recognized state, transition, consequence, continuity, proof, persistence, withholding, release, correction, and recovery.

The public proof estate combines the controlled public dossier, standing records, packet manifests, receipts, bounded artifact bindings, named declarations, historical captures, delivery boundaries, and suite verification. Each artifact establishes only the claim class and proof ceiling stated by its controlling record.

## Proof classes

### Runtime standing
The accepted 52/53 standing baseline is an EHCOnomics-controlled operational standing record. The public repository projects that record but does not create, recalculate, promote, demote, revoke, or replace it.

### Artifact identity and provenance
Hashes, manifests, revisions, filenames, and packet-time paths may establish the identity and integrity of exact artifacts. They do not establish that a repository, folder, or file collection is the Runtime or an authority location.

### Declaration and anchor presence
A packet may establish that a named declaration, class, function, route, manifest entry, or anchor exists in an identified artifact. Presence does not, by itself, establish execution, enforcement, Runtime admission, authoritative persistence, or operational consequence.

### Test and observation evidence
A test result or capture may establish behavior within its exact inputs, environment, version, time, and proof scope. It does not become universal behavioral proof or current Runtime state unless an applicable Runtime-originated record establishes that relationship.

### Runtime-originated proof
Runtime-originated state, receipts, persistence, release, revocation, recovery, and consequence records occupy a different proof class from repository files, tests, captures, dashboards, or public packets.

## Public Evidence Companion

Packets 00-08 preserve the public-safe proof estate. Packet 02 binds a packet-time set of runtime-support test and source artifacts to exact hashes. Packets 03-05 bind artifact identity and named declarations or anchors. Packet 06 preserves a historical bounded test and observation capture with explicit discrepancies and not-directly-observed lanes. Packet 08 verifies suite integrity and closure boundaries.

## Language-system position

The EHCO Language Model is a downstream governed component. Its persistent identity and capability are separate from any scoped Runtime participation relationship. Where Runtime participation is established, the applicable Runtime evidence owns admission, binding, invocation, consequence, persistence, and receipt claims.
